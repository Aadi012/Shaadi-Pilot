# Homepage assets

The two photographs are generated marketing imagery retrieved from the approved
Google Stitch homepage, not private guest gallery photos.

- Project: https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/7444919587454097178
- Screen: `612e1e18f4f54c11afcfaa7a644519c1`
- `wedding-couple.jpg`: hero photograph from the screen HTML.
- `wedding-family.jpg`: collaboration photograph from the screen HTML.

Newsreader and Manrope are served locally from `public/fonts`. Their original
SIL Open Font License notices are included beside the fonts. Source files came
from the Google Fonts repository's `ofl/newsreader` and `ofl/manrope` directories.
No font or image provider is contacted by the page at runtime or during builds.
